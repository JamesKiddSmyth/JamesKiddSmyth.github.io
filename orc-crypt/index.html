The story

    Where it stands

    Engine
    C# on .NET 10. Pure rules core with seeded dice, so any game replays exactly from its log, as this one does.
    Rounds
    Everyone declares before initiative is rolled. Ties strike at once, so a dying orc still lands his blow. Sleep lands before anyone moves.
    Tactics
    Split the party and move round by round. Monsters notice your lantern, hear the fight and join it, wander, force doors, and wait for friends in a doorway.
    Quality
    674 tests, architecture tests that keep the engine and the screen apart, and an independent review after every step.
    Next
    More monsters and the undead, morale, a second level, and monsters played by a language model.

    The party in this game was Dunstan (human fighter), Edith (human cleric), Maren (human thief) and Caladh (elf magic-user). The map is data/levels/orc-crypt.json; positions, hits and spells come from logs/game-20261001-013211-seed5372644423807541506.log.
